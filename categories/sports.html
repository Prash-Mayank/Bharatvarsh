Olympic Journey

Historic Achievements

India's Olympic journey began in 1900. Hockey dominated the early years with 8 gold medals between 1928-1980, establishing India as a sporting powerhouse.

First medal: 1900 Paris (Norman Pritchard)
Hockey golden era: 1928-1980

Modern Era

Recent Olympics have seen diversification with medals in shooting, badminton, wrestling, boxing, and athletics. Tokyo 2020 was India's best performance with 7 medals.

Tokyo 2020: 7 medals (best ever)
Neeraj Chopra: Athletics gold

Individual Excellence

Individual sports have emerged as India's strength with medals in shooting, badminton, wrestling, boxing, and athletics showcasing diverse talent.

Shooting: Abhinav Bindra (2008 gold)
Badminton: P.V. Sindhu (2016, 2020)
