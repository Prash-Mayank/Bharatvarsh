Traditional Cooking Techniques

Tandoor

Clay oven cooking at high temperatures (900°F+) for breads, meats, and vegetables. Imparts unique smoky flavor and texture.

Naan, Roti, Tandoori Chicken
5000+ years old technique

Dum Cooking

Slow cooking in sealed pots with dough, allowing ingredients to cook in their own steam and juices for enhanced flavors.

Biryani, Dum Aloo, Korma
Mughal era technique

Fermentation

Natural fermentation process for creating healthy, probiotic-rich foods with enhanced nutritional value and digestibility.

Idli, Dosa, Dhokla, Pickles
Ancient preservation method

Tempering (Tadka)

Heating spices in oil or ghee to release essential oils and flavors, then adding to dishes for aromatic enhancement.

Dal, Sambar, Rasam
Flavor enhancement technique
