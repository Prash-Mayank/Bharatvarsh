About Sikkim

Sikkim, nestled in the Eastern Himalayas, is India's second smallest state and a biodiversity hotspot. Known as the "Land of Mystical Splendor," it became India's first fully organic state in 2016, setting a global example for sustainable agriculture and environmental conservation.

The state is home to Kanchenjunga, the world's third highest peak, and boasts rich Buddhist heritage with ancient monasteries, prayer flags, and spiritual traditions. Sikkim's unique blend of Nepali, Bhutia, and Lepcha cultures creates a harmonious multicultural society in the lap of the Himalayas.

Culture & Traditions

Festivals

Losar (Tibetan New Year), Saga Dawa, Pang Lhabsol, Dashain, and Tihar celebrations.

Buddhist Heritage

Ancient monasteries, prayer wheels, stupas, and meditation practices in Himalayan Buddhism.

Traditional Dress

Bakhu for women, Gho for men, and traditional Lepcha and Bhutia costumes.
