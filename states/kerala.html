About Kerala

Kerala, known as "God's Own Country," is a narrow strip of coastal territory on India's southwestern tip. With the Arabian Sea on one side and the Western Ghats on the other, the state is blessed with unparalleled natural beauty featuring palm-lined beaches, serene backwaters, and lush hill stations.

The state boasts the highest literacy rate in India (94%+), highest Human Development Index in India, and is renowned worldwide as the birthplace of Ayurveda. Kerala's unique matrilineal traditions, classical art forms like Kathakali and Mohiniyattam, and its spice trade legacy make it a cultural treasure of India.

Culture & Traditions

Festivals

Onam (harvest festival), Thrissur Pooram (temple festival), Vishu (New Year), and boat races during monsoon.

Classical Arts

Kathakali dance-drama, Mohiniyattam, Kalaripayattu martial art, and Theyyam ritual performances.

Traditional Dress

Kasavu saree (white with golden border), Mundu for men, and Set-Mundu for women.
