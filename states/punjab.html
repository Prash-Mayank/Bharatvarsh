About Punjab

Punjab, meaning "Land of Five Rivers," derives its name from the five tributaries of the Indus River that flow through the region. Known as India's breadbasket, Punjab is the leading producer of wheat and rice, contributing significantly to the nation's food security.

The state is the spiritual and cultural center of Sikhism, home to the magnificent Golden Temple in Amritsar. Punjab's rich heritage includes vibrant folk traditions, energetic Bhangra dance, soulful music, and a warrior spirit forged through centuries of history. The warmth and hospitality of Punjabi people is legendary, as is their zest for life.

Culture & Traditions

Festivals

Vaisakhi (harvest festival), Lohri, Guru Nanak Jayanti, Diwali, and Hola Mohalla.

Dance & Music

Bhangra and Giddha folk dances, dhol drums, Punjabi pop music, and Sufi traditions.

Traditional Dress

Colorful Punjabi suits (salwar kameez), turban (pagri/dastar), and phulkari embroidery.
