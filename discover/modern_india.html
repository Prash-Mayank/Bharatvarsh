Space Achievements

ISRO - Indian Space Research Organisation

Founded in 1969, ISRO has achieved remarkable milestones in space exploration, from launching satellites to Mars missions, establishing India as a major space power.

Known for cost-effective missions, ISRO's Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) cost less than a Hollywood movie, making India the first nation to reach Mars in its first attempt.

Economic Reforms

The 1991 economic liberalization marked India's transition from a socialist economy to a market-oriented one, unleashing entrepreneurship and foreign investment.

Recent initiatives like GST implementation, Make in India, and Atmanirbhar Bharat have further strengthened India's position as a global economic powerhouse.

1991: Economic Liberalization
2014: Make in India Initiative
2017: GST Implementation
