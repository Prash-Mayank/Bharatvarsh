About South India

South India represents the heartland of Dravidian civilization, encompassing five states and two Union Territories that showcase India's ancient heritage alongside modern technological prowess. This region is renowned for its magnificent temple architecture, classical performing arts, diverse languages, and as the global hub of India's IT revolution.

From the backwaters of Kerala to the tech corridors of Bangalore, from the ancient temples of Tamil Nadu to the film industry of Hyderabad, South India presents a fascinating blend of tradition and modernity. The region's contributions to literature, science, philosophy, and arts have shaped Indian culture for over two millennia.

Notable Personalities

South India has produced exceptional scientists, musicians, mathematicians, and leaders who have left an indelible mark on India and the world.

C.V. Raman

Tamil Nadu

1888-1970

First Asian Nobel laureate in Physics. Discovered the Raman Effect, revolutionizing understanding of light scattering. Bharat Ratna recipient.

A.R. Rahman

Tamil Nadu

Born 1967

Oscar-winning composer known as "Mozart of Madras." His music blends Indian classical with world genres. Won two Academy Awards for Slumdog Millionaire.

Srinivasa Ramanujan

Tamil Nadu

1887-1920

Self-taught mathematical genius who made extraordinary contributions to number theory. His work continues to influence mathematics today.

N.R. Narayana Murthy

Karnataka

Born 1946

Co-founder of Infosys, pioneer of India's IT revolution. Transformed Bangalore into a global tech hub. Padma Vibhushan and Padma Shri awardee.

M.S. Subbulakshmi

Tamil Nadu

1916-2004

Legendary Carnatic vocalist, first musician awarded Bharat Ratna. Her rendition of devotional songs transcended boundaries and touched millions.

APJ Abdul Kalam

Tamil Nadu

1931-2015

"Missile Man of India" and 11th President. Aerospace scientist who inspired youth nationwide. Bharat Ratna recipient and beloved people's president.

Krishnadevaraya

Karnataka

1471-1529

Greatest ruler of Vijayanagara Empire. Patron of arts and literature, his reign is considered the golden age of South Indian history.

Rukmini Devi Arundale

Tamil Nadu

1904-1986

Revived Bharatanatyam classical dance and founded Kalakshetra. Her work elevated dance from entertainment to respected art form. Padma Bhushan awardee.
