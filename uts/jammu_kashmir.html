About Jammu & Kashmir

Jammu & Kashmir, often called "Paradise on Earth," became a Union Territory on October 31, 2019. This region encompasses the breathtaking Kashmir valley with its pristine lakes and snow-capped mountains, and the spiritual land of Jammu with its ancient temples and diverse topography.

The territory is renowned for its natural beauty, from the houseboats of Dal Lake to the pilgrimage sites of Vaishno Devi. With a rich cultural tapestry woven from Hindu, Muslim, and Buddhist traditions, J&K represents India's incredible diversity while holding strategic importance in the northern frontier.
