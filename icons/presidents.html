Presidents of India

Constitutional heads and guardians of the Republic

Guardians of the Constitution

The President of India is the constitutional head of state and the first citizen of India. Since independence, distinguished personalities from various fields have held this highest office, upholding the values of democracy and constitutional governance.

Constitutional Powers & Duties

Executive Head
Supreme Commander of Armed Forces
Legislative Role
Assent to Bills, Address Parliament
Judicial Powers
Grant Pardons, Commute Sentences

Presidents of India

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

1950-1962

1st President

First President of India, freedom fighter, and close associate of Mahatma Gandhi. Set constitutional precedents.

Background: Lawyer, Freedom Fighter
Achievement: Bharat Ratna (1962)
Born: Ziradei, Bihar
"The Constitution is not a mere lawyer's document, it is a vehicle of life."

Dr. S. Radhakrishnan

1962-1967

2nd President

Philosopher, academic, and statesman. His birthday (September 5) is celebrated as Teachers' Day in India.

Background: Philosopher, Educator
Achievement: Bharat Ratna (1954)
Legacy: Teachers' Day (Sept 5)
"Teachers should be the best minds in the country."

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

2002-2007

People's President

Renowned scientist, architect of India's missile program, and beloved "People's President" known for inspiring youth.

Background: Scientist, Engineer
Achievement: Bharat Ratna (1997)
Famous for: Missile Man of India
"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

Pratibha Patil

2007-2012

First woman President of India, lawyer, and politician from Maharashtra.

Term: 12th President
Background: Lawyer, Politician

Pranab Mukherjee

2012-2017

Veteran politician, former Finance Minister, and recipient of Bharat Ratna.

Term: 13th President
Background: Politician, Economist

Ram Nath Kovind

2017-2022

Lawyer, politician, and former Governor of Bihar. Second Dalit President of India.

Term: 14th President
Background: Lawyer, Politician

Droupadi Murmu

2022-Present

Current President, first tribal woman to hold the office, former Governor of Jharkhand.

Term: 15th President
Background: Politician, Social Worker
