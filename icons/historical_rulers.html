Legendary Rulers of India

Great Emperors and Kings

Chandragupta Maurya

340-297 BCE

Empire Builder

Founder of the Mauryan Empire, first ruler to unify most of the Indian subcontinent under one rule.

Dynasty: Mauryan Empire
Capital: Pataliputra (Patna)
Achievement: First unified Indian empire
"A king is he who has the power to protect his subjects."

Ashoka the Great

304-232 BCE

The Great

Greatest Mauryan emperor, promoted Buddhism and non-violence after the transformative Kalinga War.

Dynasty: Mauryan Empire
Religion: Buddhism promoter
Famous for: Edicts, Dhamma, Lion Capital
"Conquest by Dhamma is the best conquest."

Akbar the Great

1542-1605 CE

Akbar-e-Azam

Greatest Mughal emperor, known for religious tolerance, administrative reforms, and cultural synthesis.

Dynasty: Mughal Empire
Capital: Fatehpur Sikri, Agra
Famous for: Din-i Ilahi, Religious tolerance
"A monarch should be ever intent on conquest, lest his neighbors rise in arms against him."

Chhatrapati Shivaji

1630-1680 CE

Founder of the Maratha Empire, master of guerrilla warfare and naval strategy.

Dynasty: Maratha Empire
Capital: Raigad

Harsha Vardhana

590-647 CE

Last great Hindu emperor of northern India, patron of arts and Buddhism.

Dynasty: Pushyabhuti
Capital: Kanauj

Krishnadevaraya

1471-1529 CE

Greatest ruler of the Vijayanagara Empire, patron of arts, literature, and architecture.

Dynasty: Vijayanagara Empire
Capital: Vijayanagara
